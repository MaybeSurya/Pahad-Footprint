Methodology & Sources

Calculation framework, data sources, and illustrative assumptions

Illustrative Methodology Notice: All emission factors, terrain multipliers, and accommodation intensities are illustrative sample values used for prototype demonstration. They are not verified against official scientific databases and should not be used for certified carbon reporting or offset claims.

1. Transport Emission Calculation

For vehicle-based modes (SUV, Sedan, EV):
kg CO₂e/person = (distance_km × emission_factor_g × terrain_multiplier) ÷ (passengers × 1000)
For shared public modes (UTC Bus):
kg CO₂e/person = (distance_km × emission_factor_g × terrain_multiplier) ÷ 1000
